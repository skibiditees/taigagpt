# Republic of Taiga — Country Knowledge

## 1. Overview

The Republic of Taiga is a democratic republic and a community/nation whose purpose is to manage and improve life for the people who call Taiga home. Its approximate population in the supplied overview is **240 citizens and 20 migrants**.

The national flag is described as a tricolor featuring **green, navy blue, brown, and dark brown**, with a **snowflake in the middle**. The source calls it a tricolor while naming four colors; retain that description as supplied rather than guessing how the flag is divided.

The nation has also been referred to historically as the **New Taiga Republic**. The supplied history says the name was changed around July 5–6 after the Emerald SMP was replaced by the Insert Name SMP and following the end of TSMP Season 1. The current overview uses “Republic of Taiga.”

## 2. Government and current officeholders

Taiga is a **democratic republic**. Governmental authority derives from the citizens of the Taiga Nation.

The government structure includes:
- President(s)
- Ministers responsible for Diplomacy; Economy and Business (formerly Trade and Labour); Defense; Police; Infrastructure; and Agriculture
- A Chief Justice
- High Court Judges
- District Court Judges

## 4. Citizen rights

Every citizen has the right to:
- Vote in public elections and referendums.
- Receive fair treatment under the law.
- Express opinions and criticize the government.
- Freedom of speech.
- Access all freely available information.
- Receive due process.
- Participate in and run for any position in all forms of government.
- Propose motions.
- Freedom of religion.

No citizen may be punished for their political opinions, criticism, or participation in elections and votes.

All citizens have the right to a trial by a jury of peers and an unbiased lawyer.

## 5. Immigration and residency

Any person may apply for residency in the Taiga Nation through the official immigration process.

The **Minister of Diplomacy (MoD)** has full authority over who is accepted as an immigrant and who becomes a full citizen.

An applicant must wait at least **one week after being accepted** before applying for full citizenship.

Immigrants who are not full citizens may:
- Live within the Taiga Nation.
- Participate in community activities.

Residents who are not full citizens may not:
- Vote in elections.
- Vote in referendums.
- Run for government office.

Citizens who also hold citizenship in another nation may keep dual citizenship.

## 6. Judiciary

Judicial power belongs to the courts. The Taiga Judicial System is described as consisting of:
- 1 Chief Justice of Taiga
- 2 High Court Judges
- 3 District Court Judges

The supplied constitution says that the **Supreme Court will be the Chief Justice** and that the Supreme Court has the highest authority in the Taiga Judicial System. This wording is preserved as supplied; the source does not further explain the relationship between the Chief Justice and the Supreme Court.

## 7. Elections

All legal and registered citizens may cast **one vote per account per election**.

Any full citizen may run for office unless stopped by Judges.

Candidates may run individually or as part of a group with one or more other candidates, where permitted by the office being sought. This group-candidacy rule does **not** apply to judicial elections.

The supplied history says elections were on hold at the time of that account because the co-presidents had been elected to serve for two months after the server's start, but the server had been delayed. Some members of the government were questioning whether they still wanted to serve. This is a historical status report and should not be assumed to describe the situation today.

## 8. Constitutional amendments

### Proposal

An amendment may be proposed by:
- The combined authority of three ministers; or
- Two ministers together with a president.

Amendments may also be proposed by a motion or by a two-thirds supermajority vote of the citizens.

### Approval

An amendment requires a public vote and at least a **two-thirds supermajority** for approval.

## 9. Citizens' authority

Citizens are the ultimate source of governmental authority in Taiga.

A lawful citizen vote has greater authority than any individual government official, ministry, court, or governmental body.

All branches of government must respect and enforce the results of lawful citizen votes.

## 10. History of the Republic

### Early formation and temporary leadership

At the beginning, when nation selectors were announced, participants were assigned to the Taiga biome. The first plan was to keep most people in the Taiga chat on the DESMP server and create a separate Discord server with only around 10 council members. The plan later changed, and the wider community was invited into the Discord server.

The community agreed that a temporary leader was needed before a proper election. A small vote appointed **BillieCheese** as temporary leader.

The early period was chaotic. Some people claimed government or military positions without having been appointed; the account gives **Suke claiming to be a military general** as an example. BillieCheese later became inactive. Community members called for elections, but they were repeatedly delayed until the server launch. The historical account characterizes the responses to those demands as authoritarian and rude.

### First Taigan general election

After frustration with the delays, community members held an election themselves. There were more than 13 candidates. **Jan** was a prominent candidate, and campaign posters appeared, including one reading “Billiecheese is not my lover.”

The account says chaos and “witch hunting” were common during this period.

Jan won with **54.2%** of the vote. The recorded results were:

At the time, the Taiga Discord had around 80 people, and 50 people voted. This explains why the highest candidate total was 26.

### The Snowfox operation and Lukie controversy

After becoming president, Jan began the **Snowfox operation**, which involved sending spies across biomes.

A prominent spy known as **Lukie** had repeatedly criticized Taiga and taken things out of context. Lukie somehow joined the Snowfox Discord server and leaked its plans publicly in DESMP.

The leak caused public anger because many Taigans felt Jan had not been transparent about the operation. The account also points to the limited participation in the election: Taiga had around 80 Discord members, but only 50 had voted.

As a result, President Jan and Minister of Defense Moth were re-elected / their positions were put to another election, according to the supplied account. The Minister of Defense position was won by **ThunderLOL**.

### Second general election and co-presidency

A later general election became a tie between two groups:
- **PB&J**, associated with Jan, Blockrates, and Pototoes.
- **S&D**, associated with Spinny and Dodo.

A further election was held between the groups. **S&D won**, and **Spinny and Dodo became co-presidents**.

The Minister of Defense position was contested again because ThunderLOL did not have enough time to serve due to real-life problems. **Bobby** became the new Minister of Defense.

The supplied overview separately lists Dodo and Spinny as the presidents as of September 26, 2026.

### Name change

Around **July 5–6** (the source says times were in UTC+2), Taiga discussed and voted on changing its name to the **New Taiga Republic**. The stated context was the replacement of Emerald SMP by Insert Name SMP and the end of TSMP Season 1.

The current overview calls the nation the Republic of Taiga, while this historical section records the earlier “New Taiga Republic” name.

### Presidential history and controversies

The supplied account identifies three presidents across Taiga's history:
1. **Jan** — the first elected president; later removed in a coup following controversies. One prominent controversy involved a Discord screenshot concerning an early figure called Lukie, which the account describes as fake.
2. **Spinny**
3. **Dodo**

Spinny and Dodo serve together as co-presidents. The account says their group won against Jan's PB&J group by only two votes.

### Constitution rewrite

The previous constitution was considered outdated. Co-president **Dodo** led a major constitutional rewrite.

Community members could apply to become **“Nerds”**, a group tasked with rewriting the constitution and drafting laws. One example of a law assigned to the group was the **Redwood Preservation Act**.

## 11. Geography

Taiga is located:
- **South of Snow**
- **West of Forest**

Redwood forests are located near the Scarlet City area. The Taiga biome is described as diverse and full of potential.

Near **Latveria**, the southernmost city, there is a mountainous region.
